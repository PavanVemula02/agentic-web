Shankarpalle, Telangana

A quiet weekend stay near Hyderabad.

Anantha Weekend Stay is a fictional countryside property created for the Open Agentic Web Challenge. Visitors can explore accommodation, compare prices and test an agent-assisted reservation experience.

Hackathon demonstration only. No real reservation is created, no payment is collected, and room availability is simulated. Demo bookings remain only in this browser tab until the page is refreshed.

Rooms and rates

Both accommodations include breakfast and Wi-Fi. The Garden Suite provides step-free access, while the Family Cottage offers additional space, a kitchen and a private patio.

Check availability

Stays that include a Saturday night require at least two nights. The maximum simulated stay is 14 nights.
